## Projects

### 1. Electric Vehicle Data Analysis – Tableau Dashboard
Interactive dashboard exploring EV adoption across the U.S. by model year, vehicle type, make, state and electric range.
- KPI views for 70,204 vehicles, 136.2-mile average electric range and BEV/PHEV split
- Trend, map, brand ranking and model-level views for quick interpretation

**Tools:** Tableau, KPI reporting, geographic analysis

### 2. Streaming Platforms BI Strategy – Tableau & BI Roadmap
Case study for Salt Productions comparing Amazon, Netflix, Hulu and Disney+ content libraries.
- Analysed content volume, release-year trends, geography and ratings
- Recommended BI capabilities: data integration, governance, predictive analytics and personalisation
- Turned findings into recommendations on acquisition, localisation and recommendation systems

**Tools:** Tableau, BI strategy, data visualisation, analytics roadmapping

### 3. Vrinda Store Data Analysis – Retail / E-commerce
Analysis of 31,047 retail order records covering customer demographics, order status, channel, category and location.
- Cleaned and structured the dataset for analysis
- Compared sales by channel, category, status, gender and state
- INR 21.18m total sales, with Amazon, Myntra and Flipkart as top channels

**Tools:** Excel, data cleaning, pivot analysis, dashboarding

### 4. Graduate Numbers & Outcomes Dashboard
Excel workbook and dashboard exploring higher-education graduate numbers and outcomes (2010–2019) by year, region, field of study and employment.
- Worked with 26,401 graduate-number and 105,601 graduate-outcome records
- Multi-chart dashboard with slicers for year and field of study

**Tools:** Excel, pivot tables, pivot charts, slicers, data modelling

### 5. R Data Cleaning & Visualisation
Practice projects in R covering data preparation, transformation, exploratory analysis and visualisation.

**Tools:** R, summary statistics, data visualisation
